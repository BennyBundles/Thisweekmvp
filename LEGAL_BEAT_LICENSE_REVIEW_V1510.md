# TAGJ Beat Licensing V15.10 — Legal / Product Review

**Review date:** 2026-09-19  
**Reviewer:** GPT-5.6 Sol, structured legal-risk review grounded in U.S. Copyright Office and Music Modernization Act / MLC materials  
**Not reviewed by:** a connected "Legal GPT" plugin or a licensed attorney  
**Status:** DRAFTS IMPROVED; ATTORNEY REVIEW STILL REQUIRED BEFORE PAID CHECKOUT

## Executive result

The five license templates are materially stronger after V15.10. They now separate:
- the Beat's musical-composition rights from its sound-recording rights;
- technical audio review from legal sample clearance;
- original DAW trackouts from stereo-derived separation stems;
- genuine lossless masters from WAV containers decoded from MP3;
- composition ownership splits from separately administered mechanical/performance royalty systems;
- ordinary non-exclusive licenses from the signed-writing requirements and prior-license issues created by an exclusive license.

The agreements remain suitable for **draft terms / inquiry use**, not attorney-approved checkout terms.

## Authorities used for the review

1. U.S. Copyright Office, 17 U.S.C. § 204 — transfers of copyright ownership must be in a writing signed by the owner or authorized agent.
2. U.S. Copyright Office musician guidance — musical works and sound recordings are separate protected works and may be separately owned/licensed.
3. U.S. Copyright Office sampling / beat-store guidance — use of preexisting music can require permission depending on the rights implicated; there is no universal minimum amount that is automatically safe.
4. The Mechanical Licensing Collective materials — mechanical royalties concern reproduction/distribution of musical works and U.S. eligible interactive streaming/download mechanicals are administered through the statutory system.

## Findings

### 1. Exclusive-license execution — PASS WITH FINAL IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT

The Exclusive template is correctly treated as an exclusive license rather than an automatic transfer of the entire underlying Beat copyright. Because an exclusive license transfers an exclusive copyright right, the final transaction should retain a signed writing identifying the Beat and rights granted. The present draft requires signature and retains prior valid leases.

**Action before checkout:** implement an auditable signed/e-signed agreement record containing agreement version, Beat ID, rights granted, price, parties, timestamp and signature/assent evidence.

### 2. Composition vs sound recording — PASS

The templates correctly distinguish the underlying composition from the Beat sound recording. This matters for samples, interpolations, publishing splits and clearances.

### 3. Sample / interpolation clearance — IMPROVED; BEAT-BY-BEAT ATTESTATION STILL REQUIRED

Technical audio review cannot prove chain of title. V15.10 therefore adds:
- a 22-row sample/provenance review;
- a Producer Rights / Sample Declaration;
- a licensing activation rule requiring provenance documentation;
- Beat-specific holds.

No Beat is currently represented as legally sample-cleared solely because its stereo master was reviewed.

### 5. MP3 → WAV conversion — PASS WITH DISCLOSURE

Four 24-bit / 48 kHz PCM WAV derivatives were created:
- Four Eights
- Basement Gold
- Long Way Home
- Daylight Run

They remain **MP3-origin derivatives**. The contracts and catalogue now expressly state that decoding an MP3 into WAV does not restore the discarded information and does not create a genuine lossless master.

**Result:** these files may be used as standardized WAV delivery/editing containers, but Premium/Unlimited/Exclusive tiers must not promise a genuine lossless source master until one is supplied.

### 6. Stems / Trackout — PASS WITH PRODUCT RESTRICTION

A three-part derived mix-control pack was generated for all 22 Beats:
- drums/percussion derived;
- bass derived;
- music/harmonic derived.

These are algorithmically separated from the stereo master. They are useful editing assets but are **not original DAW multitracks**.

The Trackout Pro agreement now explicitly distinguishes original DAW trackouts from Derived Mix-Control Stems.

**Result:** the presence of the 22 derived packs does not automatically activate Trackout Pro as an original-trackout product. Either:
1. supply original DAW stems; or
2. expressly contract for a derived-stem product in Schedule A with the buyer's acceptance of the disclosure.

### 7. Publishing / mechanical / performance administration — IMPROVED

The templates now clarify that the contract's composition split does not waive or replace royalty systems administered through applicable PROs, The MLC, publishers, administrators, DSPs and distributors. Metadata must preserve the agreed ownership information.

This is preferable to implying that a one-time Beat license fee extinguishes the producer/songwriter side's separately administered composition royalties.

### 8. Content ID — PASS

Non-exclusive tiers continue to prohibit unilateral Content ID / Rights Manager ownership claims without permission. Exclusive Content ID remains subject to written clearance and whitelisting procedures because prior valid non-exclusive releases may survive.

### 9. Prior leases vs later exclusive — PASS

The Exclusive template states that existing valid non-exclusive licenses survive a later exclusive sale. This reduces the risk of an exclusive purchaser attempting to takedown earlier authorized songs.

### 10. Capacity / signing authority — IMPROVED

The templates now require signers to represent legal capacity and authority to bind any company/label/entity they sign for.

## Required activation sequence

Before paid licensing is turned on for any Beat:

1. Complete and sign the Producer Rights / Sample Declaration.
2. Resolve any Beat-specific provenance flag.
3. Confirm the LLC's authority to license both the rights actually being offered.
4. Attach third-party permissions / production-library license evidence where applicable.
5. Confirm the correct delivery-source status.
6. Use original DAW trackouts for Trackout Pro, or expressly sell a disclosed derived-stem product instead.
7. Have a qualified attorney review the final templates and checkout/e-sign flow.

## Product-state conclusion

**Safe current state:** audition → review draft terms → submit inquiry.

**Not yet approved state:** automatic payment → automatic license issuance.

That restriction should remain in code until the rights declarations and attorney review are complete.
