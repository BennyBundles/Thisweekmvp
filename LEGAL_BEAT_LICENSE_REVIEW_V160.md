## Official legal-source review

1. U.S. Copyright Office — What Musicians Should Know About Copyright
   https://www.copyright.gov/engage/musicians/
   - musical works and sound recordings are separate protected works;
   - use of another party's work generally requires permission, a valid license, public-domain status, or an applicable statutory limitation/exception;
   - there is no automatic minimum amount of music that is always safe to copy without permission.

2. U.S. Copyright Office — Sampling, Interpolations, Beat Stores and More
   https://www.copyright.gov/music-modernization/educational-materials/Sampling-Interpolations-Beat-Stores-and-More-An-Introduction-for-Musicians-Using-Preexisting.pdf
   - sampling incorporates part of a preexisting sound recording into a new work;
   - a sample may implicate sound-recording rights and underlying musical-work rights.

3. Copyright Act, 17 U.S.C. §§ 101, 204
   https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html
   https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html
   - an exclusive license is included in a transfer of copyright ownership;
   - a transfer of copyright ownership must be in writing and signed by the rights owner or authorized agent.

4. Pennsylvania Department of State — Foreign Associations
   https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dos/programs/business/types-of-filings-and-registrations/foreign-associations
   - a foreign LLC may register to do business in Pennsylvania;
   - registration can subject the company to general personal jurisdiction in Pennsylvania.

## MP3 → WAV replacement review

Four original catalogue sources were MP3-only:
- beat-009 / Four Eights
- beat-017 / Basement Gold
- beat-018 / Long Way Home
- beat-019 / Daylight Run

WAV delivery derivatives were created and uploaded to Drive. The original MP3s were preserved as separate Drive backup copies before the catalogue was switched to the new WAV file IDs.

**Legal/product disclosure:** decoding an MP3 into PCM WAV changes the delivery container but cannot restore information discarded by the original MP3 encoding. Contracts and catalogue metadata now say this explicitly.

## Trackout review

- 22/22 beats have completed derived separation packs.
- Each pack contains drums/percussion, bass, and music/harmonic stems.
- Delivery format: 24-bit lossless FLAC.
- Packs were derived algorithmically from stereo masters and are **not original DAW multitracks**.
- All three stems are non-silent.
- Separation reconstruction checks passed at near-floating-point reconstruction error before delivery quantization.
- Common output gain was applied where needed to avoid stem clipping.
- The Trackout Pro agreement now expressly discloses possible separation bleed/artifacts.
- The public product name is changed to **Trackout Pro — Derived 3-Stem**.
