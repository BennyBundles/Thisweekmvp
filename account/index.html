Data boundary

What deleting means

Browser-local Plan data and the cloud identity are separate. Deleting the cloud account does not silently delete local Plan data on other devices.

No retained money history: the cloud identity can be hard-deleted.
Financial history exists: deletion becomes a closure/review request so accounting records are not silently destroyed.
