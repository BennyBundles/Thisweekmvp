PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

Useful before flashy.

01 / DECISION SUPPORTAssist, don’t replace.

The tool should accelerate melodic exploration while leaving selection, taste, arrangement and final musical judgment with the producer.

02 / MIDI-FIRSTUse your sounds.

Prioritize musical structure, editable MIDI and portability before adding a heavy proprietary sound library.

03 / LOW FRICTIONFree-first entry.

Reduce adoption friction, get the tool into real producer sessions, and use feedback before investing in paid infrastructure.

04 / HONEST RELEASENo fake download.

No public installer is represented until the build has an actual version, supported platform target, install path, integrity check and support route.
