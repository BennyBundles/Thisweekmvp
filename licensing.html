IMPORTANT DELIVERY + RIGHTS DISTINCTIONS

What the license does not overstate.

Audio qualityMP3-origin WAV is not lossless

A WAV container created from an MP3 source does not restore the audio information removed by MP3 compression. Those files are disclosed as MP3-origin derivatives.

TrackoutsDerived stems are not DAW multitracks

The current 3-stem trackout packs were separated from stereo masters. They may contain bleed or separation artifacts and are not represented as original session stems.

RightsProducer attestation

Benjamin Alexander has represented that all 22 catalogue beats are original, unsold and not previously exclusively licensed. That representation is recorded in the catalogue; it is not described as an independent title opinion from counsel.

Content IDNon-exclusive claims are restricted

Non-exclusive customers may not create unilateral ownership/fingerprinting conflicts that interfere with other legitimate licensees.
