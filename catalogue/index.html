This is the development catalogue, not a fake storefront. A product can be visually developed without being manufactured, stocked or available to buy. Pricing and checkout appear only after inventory or made-to-order status is explicitly confirmed.

Commerce boundary: catalogue inclusion does not mean a product is in stock or for sale. Current product records maintain separate development, inventory and commerce fields so concept status cannot accidentally become a purchase claim.
